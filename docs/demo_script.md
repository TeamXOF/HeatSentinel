### 📍 STAGE 1: Problem Framing & Multi-City Overview (0:00 - 0:35)
- **Screen:** `Overview Page (/)`
- **Actions:**
  1. Open app, point to top KPI banner: **Peak 114.1°F, 16,568 Cells, 12,866 Monitored Population**.
  2. Click the City Selector in the header to demonstrate multi-metro coverage (Phoenix, Las Vegas, Miami, Houston, Los Angeles, New York).
- **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Extreme urban heat kills more Americans each year than hurricanes, tornadoes, and floods combined. But today, cities rely on coarse regional weather stations located at distant airports—missing 15°F surface heat spikes that turn concrete corridors into lethal ovens.*
  > *This is HeatSentinel AI: an autonomous hyperlocal heat response command center monitoring top US metros in real time."*

---

### 📍 STAGE 2: Hyperlocal 60m Thermal Grid (0:35 - 1:15)
- **Screen:** `Heat Map (/heat-map)`
- **Actions:**
  1. Switch to Heat Map page.
  2. Click on the **Maryvale & West** or **Downtown Core** district preset to demonstrate smooth 3D camera fly-in.
  3. Click anywhere on the map to drop a custom target marker and watch the real-time geometric polygon hull buffer render.
- **Spoken Script:**
  > *"By integrating FortyGuard's 60-meter satellite thermal mesh, HeatSentinel ingests over 16,000 hyperlocal grid cells. Instead of a single city temperature, we see exact micro-heat islands.*
  > *Notice that emergency operators can click any district or draw custom AOI buffers to instantly inspect asphalt thermal retention and tree canopy deficits on specific blocks."*

---

### 📍 STAGE 3: Autonomous Heat Hunt Agent (1:15 - 2:00)
- **Screen:** `Agent Insights (/agent-insights)`
- **Actions:**
  1. Click **Run Heat Hunt** in the top header.
  2. Watch the live Server-Sent Events (SSE) stream populate the Agent Activity Panel through its 7-phase loop.
- **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Rather than forcing city staff to manually parse gigabytes of raster data, HeatSentinel deploys an autonomous AI agent.*
  > *Our 7-phase Heat Hunt orchestrator automatically partitions the target area, runs DBSCAN clustering, queries FortyGuard thermal persistence, joins US Census socioeconomic vulnerability, checks municipal cooling readiness, and calculates risk scores—streaming live tool traces with zero hallucinations."*

---

### 📍 STAGE 4: Response Gap Formula & WHY Evidence Drawer (2:00 - 2:35)
- **Screen:** `Risk Zones (/risk-zones)`
- **Actions:**
  1. Click on **Zone 1 (Maryvale)** to slide open the **WHY? Evidence Drawer**.
  2. Highlight the 3 component score breakdown and data source attribution.
- **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Temperature alone doesn't cause mortality—demographic vulnerability and resource deficits do. Our deterministic Response Gap formula calculates risk using 40% Thermal Exposure, 35% Census SVI Demographics, and 25% Cooling Deficits.*
  > *Every single score is fully explainable: clicking 'WHY?' surfaces the exact census tracts, senior percentages, and distance to nearest cooling centers."*

---

### 📍 STAGE 5: Tactical Dispatch & Closing (2:35 - 3:00)
- **Screen:** `Response Planner (/response-planner)`
- **Actions:**
  1. Navigate to Response Planner.
  2. Point to the automated dispatch tasks (Hydration Vans, Misting Stations, Senior Wellness Checks).
- **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Finally, HeatSentinel converts raw satellite intelligence into immediate tactical dispatch orders—directing hydration units and mobile shade to the exact blocks that need them most.*
  > *HeatSentinel doesn't just measure heat—it predicts vulnerability and saves lives before the heat wave strikes. Thank you."*

## 🏆 Key Judging Takeaways
- **Real Data Integration:** FortyGuard 60m thermal API + US Census ACS 5-Year Demographics + MAG Heat Relief Network.
- **Autonomous Agent Quality:** 10 core tools, 7-phase loop, SSE streaming with 3-tier fallback reliability shield.
- **System Design:** FastAPI modular monolith, deterministic Python scoring engines, zero-hallucination architecture.
